吉林大学物理学院 · 鼎新博士后Dingxin Postdoctoral Researcher · College of Physics, Jilin University

研究原子核电荷性质与精密原子物理中的核结构效应。基于协变密度泛函理论与数值计算，研究核电荷分布、核体积效应及束缚电子 g 因子；早期工作涉及原子精密计算与量子行走。My research focuses on nuclear charge properties and nuclear-structure effects in precision atomic physics. I use covariant density functional theory and numerical methods to study charge distributions, finite-nuclear-size effects, and bound-electron g factors. My earlier work includes precision atomic calculations and quantum walks.

研究兴趣Research interests

原子核电荷性质Nuclear charge properties

基于协变密度泛函理论，研究核电荷分布、电荷半径与高阶径向矩，关注核子内禀电磁结构和四极形状涨落。Nuclear charge distributions, radii and higher radial moments within covariant density functional theory, with a focus on intrinsic nucleon structure and quadrupole shape fluctuations.

核结构理论 · 电荷分布Nuclear structure · Charge distributions

精密原子物理中的核结构效应Nuclear structure in atomic precision

研究类氢离子与缪原子中的核体积效应，以及束缚电子 g 因子中的核结构贡献，连接微观核计算与精密测量。Finite-nuclear-size effects in hydrogenlike ions and muonic atoms, and nuclear contributions to bound-electron g factors: connecting microscopic nuclear calculations with precision observables.

核体积效应 · 束缚电子 g 因子Finite nuclear size · Bound-electron g factors

量子行走与量子动力学Quantum walks & quantum dynamics

硕士阶段研究了 Sierpinski 分形网络上的离散时间量子行走。以此为研究基础，关注量子信息及其与现有研究的交叉可能。My master’s research included discrete-time quantum walks on Sierpinski fractal networks. This provides a foundation for exploring connections between quantum information and my current research.
